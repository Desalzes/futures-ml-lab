# ML-006 — Chronos-2, FinCast and gated fusion

**Status:** Gated. **Upstreams:** https://github.com/amazon-science/chronos-forecasting and https://github.com/vincent05r/FinCast-fts

## Unlock condition

At least two earlier methods need independently verified, nonredundant gains against the **same** simple bar baseline using comparable causal evaluation and realistic costs. Results from synthetic-only demos cannot unlock this.

## Investigation once unblocked

1. Audit pretrained forecast distribution outputs, accessible checkpoints, training dates, supported covariates/frequency, license and inference budget.
2. Adapt probabilistic forecast statistics (tail uncertainty, quantiles, volatility) into causal `FeatureRow`s; avoid future-known covariate assumptions.
3. Train only a small gating head on **out-of-fold** specialist predictions. Compare against each single expert and unweighted average; measure turnover and execution cost.
4. Evaluate calibration, time/regime instability, worst-period costs, diversity among model errors and an unchanged candidate opportunity set.

## Stop

If no earlier specialist has a credible contribution, or fusion adds none, leave the experiment blocked. Extra ensemble complexity is not an achievement by itself.
